About Me

Hello! I'm Shay. I'm deeply passionate about the open web, independent creation, and digital craftsmanship. In a digital world increasingly dominated by walled gardens and algorithmic feeds, I value slow, intentional creation. This website is my attempt to carve out a quiet, personal space online where I can share my thoughts, projects, and explorations on my own terms.

My interests span across a wide variety of domains. I'm fascinated by generative art and the intersection of code and aesthetics. I'm also on a continuous journey to understand computing fundamentals — from building a computer from the ground up in nand2tetris to diving into the depths of operating system internals. Aside from technology, I find immense joy in hands-on craft and music.

Speaking of music, I have a deep appreciation for unique acoustic folk instruments. There is something profoundly human about the sounds they produce. I am particularly captivated by the Armenian Duduk, an ancient double-reed woodwind instrument renowned for its haunting, soulful timbre that seems to resonate with centuries of history. I also love the Charango, a small Andean stringed instrument that brings a bright, percussive character to music, often evocative of high mountain landscapes. Finally, the Ocarina, an ancient vessel flute, holds a special place for me with its pure, ethereal tone that feels almost magical.
