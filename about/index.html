About Me

Hello! I'm Shay. Welcome to my corner of the web! I'm a (soon to be graduate) student, independent creator, and digital craftsman. In a digital world increasingly dominated by ads and algorithmic feeds, I value slow, intentional exploration. This website is my attempt to carve out a personal space online where I can share my thoughts, projects, and explorations on my own terms.

My interests span across a wide variety of domains. I'm fascinated by various forms of art, music, and design. I'm also on a continuous journey of learning. Aside from technology, I find immense joy in music and other creative pursuits.

Thanks for visiting my site! Please explore! I created this space as a way to express myself and connect with others who share similar interests. eastereggs are hidden throughout the site.
