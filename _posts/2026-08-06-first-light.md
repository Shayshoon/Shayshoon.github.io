# First Light

This journal is now live. I've been meaning to start writing in the open for a while — not polished essays or hot takes, but a quieter kind of writing. A log.

## Why a journal?

I wanted a place to record things that don't fit neatly into the [digital garden](/garden/) — fleeting observations, project updates, things I cooked, places I walked. The garden is for ideas that grow over time. The journal is for moments that pass.

## How it works

Each entry is a plain Markdown file. No database, no CMS, no build step. I write a `.md` file, add it to the manifest, and it appears here — rendered in your browser by [marked.js](https://github.com/markedjs/marked).

The format supports everything Markdown does:

- **Bold** and *italic* text
- [Links](https://example.com) to other places
- Images and even video embeds
- Code blocks, blockquotes, lists

> "The best time to plant a tree was twenty years ago. The second best time is now."

So here we are. First light.
