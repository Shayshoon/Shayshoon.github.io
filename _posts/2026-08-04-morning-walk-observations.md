# Morning Walk Observations

Woke up early today and walked along the Yarkon before the heat set in. Some things I noticed:

## Light

The morning sun through the eucalyptus trees creates these long, shifting patterns on the path. Everything feels amber and unhurried at that hour.

## Sounds

The dominant sound at 7am is sprinklers. Then birds. Then, gradually, traffic. There's a brief window where the park feels like it belongs to the walkers and the street cats.

## A cat

Met a cat near the bridge with one green eye and one amber eye. She was very dignified about the whole encounter. Heterochromia is more common in white cats, apparently — something about the gene that controls coat color also affecting iris pigmentation.

## On walking

I think walking without headphones is an underrated practice. Your thoughts move at the speed of your feet. Nothing needs to be optimized.
